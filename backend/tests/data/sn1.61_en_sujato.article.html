“Name overcomes everything. 1 “Nāmaṁ sabbaṁ addhabhavi, Nothing excels name. 2 nāmā bhiyyo na vijjati; Name is the one thing Nāmassa ekadhammassa, that has everything under its sway.” 3 sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti.
