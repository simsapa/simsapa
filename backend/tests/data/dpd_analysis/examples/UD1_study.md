# Analysis of: UD1

yadā have pātubhavanti dhammā,
ātāpino jhāyato brāhmaṇassa,
ath'assa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,
yato pajānāti sahetudhamman'ti. paṭhamaṃ.

### English Translation

**Translation:** When things become manifest to the ardent, meditating brahmin, then all his doubts completely vanish, since he understands phenomena with their causes.

**Literal Translation:** When indeed dhammas become manifest / to the ardent / of the meditating brahmin / then his doubts / completely disappear all / when he understands / a phenomenon with its cause. First.
