# Analysis of: UD12

yañ'ca kāmasukhaṃ loke,
yañ'c'idaṃ diviyaṃ sukhaṃ,
taṇhakkhayasukhass'ete,
kalaṃ n'āgghanti soḷasin'ti. dutiyaṃ.

### English Translation

**Translation:** Whatever sensual happiness there is in the world, and whatever divine happiness there is, these are not worth a sixteenth part of the happiness of the destruction of craving.

**Literal Translation:** And whatever sensual pleasure in the world, and whatever divine pleasure there is, of the happiness of the destruction of craving these are not worth a sixteenth part.
