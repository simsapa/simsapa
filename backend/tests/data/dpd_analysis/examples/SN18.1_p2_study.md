# Analysis of: SN18.1_p2

taṃ kiṃ maññasi, rāhula, cakkhuṃ niccaṃ vā aniccaṃ vā'ti? aniccaṃ, bhante. yaṃ pan'āniccaṃ dukkhaṃ vā taṃ sukhaṃ vā'ti? dukkhaṃ, bhante. yaṃ pan'āniccaṃ dukkhaṃ vipariṇāmadhammaṃ, kallaṃ nu taṃ samanupassituṃ, etaṃ mama, eso'ham'asmi, eso me attā'ti? no h'etaṃ, bhante. sotaṃ niccaṃ vā aniccaṃ vā'ti? aniccaṃ, bhante …. ghānaṃ niccaṃ vā aniccaṃ vā'ti? aniccaṃ, bhante … jivhā niccā vā aniccā vā'ti? aniccā, bhante … kāyo nicco vā anicco vā'ti? anicco, bhante … mano nicco vā anicco vā'ti? anicco, bhante. yaṃ pan'āniccaṃ dukkhaṃ vā taṃ sukhaṃ vā'ti? dukkhaṃ, bhante. yaṃ pan'āniccaṃ dukkhaṃ vipariṇāmadhammaṃ, kallaṃ nu taṃ samanupassituṃ, etaṃ mama, eso'ham'asmi, eso me attā'ti? no h'etaṃ, bhante.

### English Translation

**Translation:** What do you think, Rahula? Is the eye permanent or impermanent? Impermanent, venerable sir. What is impermanent, is that suffering or happiness? Suffering, venerable sir. What is impermanent, suffering, and subject to change, is it fitting to regard it as 'This is mine, I am this, this is my self'? No indeed, venerable sir. Is the ear permanent or impermanent? Impermanent, venerable sir. Is the nose permanent or impermanent? Impermanent, venerable sir. Is the tongue permanent or impermanent? Impermanent, venerable sir. Is the body permanent or impermanent? Impermanent, venerable sir. Is the mind permanent or impermanent? Impermanent, venerable sir. What is impermanent, is that suffering or happiness? Suffering, venerable sir. What is impermanent, suffering, and subject to change, is it fitting to regard it as 'This is mine, I am this, this is my self'? No indeed, venerable sir.

**Literal Translation:** Do you think, Rahula: eye permanent or impermanent or? Impermanent, sir. That which is impermanent, suffering or that happiness or? Suffering, sir. That which is impermanent, suffering, subject-to-change, proper indeed that to regard-as: this mine, I-am-this, this my self? Not indeed this, sir. Ear permanent or impermanent or? Impermanent, sir. Nose permanent or impermanent or? Impermanent, sir. Tongue permanent or impermanent or? Impermanent, sir. Body permanent or impermanent or? Impermanent, sir. Mind permanent or impermanent or? Impermanent, sir. That which is impermanent, suffering or that happiness or? Suffering, sir. That which is impermanent, suffering, subject-to-change, proper indeed that to regard-as: this mine, I-am-this, this my self? Not indeed this, sir.
