The Birth Story about What Is Unquestionable

Apaṇṇakajātaka

Ja 1 Apaṇṇakajātaka2
The Birth Story about What Is Unquestionable

In the present a layman is reproved. Keywords: Caution.

The Bodhisatta = the wise leader.

This3 discourse [1.1] {1.95} was delivered at Jetavana, see the next file & more.

“Those who have refuge found,

Shall not pass hence.”

 

In the past there was a king.

1. Apaṇṇakaṁ ṭhānam-eke,

Etad-aññāya medhāvī.

Some have an unquestionable basis. {1.97}

This discourse ended, he preached the Truths.
